## 复现

从 `PythonAPI/examples/` 运行：

```powershell
& ..\..\venv\Scripts\python.exe -m unittest discover -s highway_hybrid/tests -q
& ..\..\venv\Scripts\python.exe -m unittest highway_eval.test_evaluate highwaytest.tests.test_straight_reward_repair highwaytest.tests.test_straight_environment highwaytest.tests.test_telemetry_focus -q
& ..\..\venv\Scripts\python.exe -m highway_hybrid smoke --level 2 --steps 512 --method mutual --output highwaytest/runs/hybrid_smoke_new
& ..\..\venv\Scripts\python.exe -m highway_hybrid probe --level 2 --cases 12 --output highwaytest/runs/hybrid_probe_new
```

正式训练和独立评估命令见 [highway_hybrid README](../../highway_hybrid/README.md)。正式效果需在固定训练预算和独立场景下另行验证。
